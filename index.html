What it holds

Four sections, one lock

Everything is stored on the phone and unlocked together. There is no per-item password to remember and nothing to sync.

Documents

CV, ID, passport

Scan with the camera or import a PDF. Both sides of a CNIC stay together as one item with two pages.

Passwords

Logins you stop retyping

A generator for strong ones, search across titles and usernames, and a clipboard that wipes itself after thirty seconds.

Cards

Bank & ID numbers

Card numbers stay masked until you choose to look, so a glance over your shoulder shows nothing.

Notes

The things you text yourself

Wi-Fi codes, account numbers, the safe combination — out of your messages and into the vault.

Where it lives

There is no server to break into

The vault never leaves your phone. That is not a setting — there is no account to make and nowhere for the data to go.

  • Encrypted with AES-256, with the key held in the phone’s own keystore.
  • Nothing is uploaded. No analytics, no adverts, no advertising ID.
  • Screenshots are blocked and the vault hides itself in the app switcher.
  • Backup is free — an encrypted file you keep, because losing everything shouldn’t be the paid part.
