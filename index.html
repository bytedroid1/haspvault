What's inside

Four sections, one lock

Everything is encrypted the same way and opens with the same PIN. No separate passwords to remember for separate things.

Documents

Scan with the camera or import what you already have, and keep both sides of a CNIC as one document with two pages. Group them into folders with Pro. Images open inside the app, so a passport never has to be handed to another app to be viewed.

Passwords

Logins with a built-in generator for strong ones, search across titles and usernames, and a clipboard that wipes itself thirty seconds after you copy. With Pro, sort them into folders like College or Work.

Cards

Bank and ID cards drawn as real cards, with the number masked until you choose to reveal it and the CVV hidden by default.

Notes

Private text for the things that don't fit a form — recovery phrases, account numbers, the answer to that security question.

How it stays private

There is no server to break into

Most apps promise security. This one removes the thing that usually fails: there is no account, no sync and nowhere for your files to be stored except your own phone.

Encrypted where it sits

Passwords, cards, notes and document details live in an AES-256 database whose key is generated on your device and held in the Android Keystore.

  • Your PIN is never stored — only a salted PBKDF2 hash
  • Wrong attempts trigger escalating lockouts
  • Auto-locks when idle, and the moment you leave the app
  • Hidden from the app switcher; screenshots blocked

Two ways out, both yours

Data only moves when you tell it to, and never through anyone else's infrastructure.

  • Backup — one encrypted file, sealed with a passphrase only you know. Free, deliberately.
  • Phone to phone — sends the vault straight to your new handset over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted, with a one-shot code that expires in three minutes.

Uninstalling deletes everything

That is the design, not an oversight. There is no cloud copy to restore from and no reset code — which is exactly why nobody else can get in either. Export a backup before you change phones.

Free, with an optional upgrade

The free version stores as much as you like — unlimited documents, passwords, cards and notes — and includes encrypted backup, because protecting yourself from data loss should never be the paid part. There are no adverts anywhere in the app.

Pro adds folders for documents and passwords, expiry reminders for your passport, CNIC or licence, a password health check, phone-to-phone transfer between iPhone and Android, break-in alerts and extra themes. Take it yearly — with a free trial when Google Play offers you one — or pay once for lifetime and never pay again.
