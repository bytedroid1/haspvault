Where it lives

There is no server to break into

Your documents, passwords and cards never leave your phone. That is not a setting you switch on — there is no account to make and nowhere for them to go.

  • Encrypted with AES-256, with the key held in the phone’s own keystore.
  • Screenshots are blocked and the vault hides itself in the app switcher.
  • Locks the moment you look away — on idle, and when you switch apps.
  • Backup is free, an encrypted file you keep. Losing everything shouldn’t be the paid part.

How it works

Three minutes to move in

Set a PIN

Four digits, plus Face ID if you want it. No email, no password, no verification code — the vault exists only on this phone.

Scan or import

Photograph a document with the camera, or bring in a PDF or an image you already have. Multi-page items stay together.

Delete the originals

Clear them out of your camera roll and your chat threads. That is the point — one copy, in one place, locked.
